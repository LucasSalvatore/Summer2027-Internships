## RBS timeline — corrected Oct 21, 2026

> [!success] RBS Fall 2027, not Fall 2028 — confirmed in writing
> Christine Yannuzzi (RBS enrollment manager), Oct 14, 2026: *"Students can apply to RBS after completing the Spring semester at SAS (completing a minimum of **12 credits**)."*
>
> Path: Spring 2027 SAS (12+ credits) → apply Feb 1 – Apr 30, 2027 → **RBS Fall 2027**. The 24-credit / Fall 2028 rule previously treated as settled is superseded. This puts you inside RBS for the Summer 2028 recruiting cycle.
>
> Math gate: *"Students must complete Precalculus or Calculus, **or place into Calculus through the math placement exam**, by the end of Spring 2027."* The placement exam is a third route — attempt it before burning a Spring seat on a math course.
>
> Honors College is closed (Oct 14: *"we only admit rising sophomores from additional institutions"*). RBS has **Beta Gamma Sigma** as its honor society. Scarlet SMF is honors-gated and therefore out; the standard LIBOR → SMF path is unaffected.
