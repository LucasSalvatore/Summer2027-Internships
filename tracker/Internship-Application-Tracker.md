## Standing constraints

> [!danger] Legal name
> All applications, portals, offer paperwork, background checks and the **Rutgers acceptance** use **Joshua Bracero** until SSA processing completes. Preferred-name fields only for "Luke Salvatore."

> [!warning] Class standing
> You are a **credit junior, Class of 2029**. Report both truthfully. Grad-date-banded programmes: eligible. Standing-gated programmes ("must be in sophomore standing"): not eligible, don't apply — offers run enrollment verification.

> [!tip] Read the band, not the firm
> Related posts the same Summer Analyst title in the same office under two different bands: *"sophomore or junior, Dec 2027–May 2029"* (yours) and *"junior, Dec 2027–Spring 2028"* (not yours). Evercore's band includes you where Morgan Stanley's near-identical wording does not, because MS joins it to a standing requirement and Evercore doesn't. Moelis Houston works for the same reason Evercore does — a band with no standing clause. Perella Weinberg may fail for the Morgan Stanley reason; nobody can tell until the sentence is read. **The eligibility sentence decides, every time.**

> [!tip] And read the source of the date, not just the date
> A deadline is only as good as who published it. Firm-stated dates (Evercore Nov 15, Moelis Dec 14, Rutgers Dec 7) are load-bearing. Dates that come from aggregators and university relays — Related's Nov 6, WayUp's "no longer accepting applications" — are weak evidence in **both** directions, and get treated as working assumptions rather than facts. The asymmetry decides what to do about them: act early on a soft deadline that might be real, and don't abandon an application on a soft closure that might be stale.

> [!warning] Spring 2027 job conflict
> Leasing work is weekend-heavy and collides with the Spring 2027 course load — the same semester that produces the recruiting transcript **and** the RBS gate GPA. Three-way conflict, and the ICSC/CREFC student memberships add a fourth (both require "not employed full time"). Settle terms before accepting any offer.
