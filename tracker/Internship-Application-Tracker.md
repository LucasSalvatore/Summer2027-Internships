## Fields worth adding — my recommendation

> [!success] 1. Real estate private equity / CRE capital markets — highest ROI
> You hold an active RE salesperson license. In an IB pool you are one of a thousand 3.5 GPAs; in RE finance you are a licensed practitioner with an RBS Real Estate concentration and an SMF REGL desk seat. **Weight this lane hardest.** It also compounds directly with the existing leasing-agent income plan.

> [!success] 2. Commercial real estate debt
> Maps exactly onto Commercial Debt Markets (33:851:390, Fall 2028) and Essentials of RE Finance (Fall 2027). Lenders, debt funds, agency shops. Less crowded than equity.

> [!success] 3. Wealth management at RIAs / private banks
> Life & health producer license is a genuine credential here — insurance is part of the WM product stack, and it's the on-ramp to the CFP track. Nobody else in the applicant pool has it.

> [!info] 4. Fintech / payments strategy
> Amex, Fiserv, Marqeta, Block. NJ/NY-dense, finance-adjacent, less GPA-brutal than IB.

> [!warning] 5. Reality check on private equity
> **Undergrad direct-entry PE is close to nonexistent.** The standard path is IB or consulting analyst → PE associate, recruited 1–2 years in. MLT's PE Accelerator requires being on track for a full-time offer in IB, VC, asset management, or consulting. Keep PE as the destination; do not build the undergrad plan around direct PE seats. The realistic undergrad-PE exceptions are Blackstone, Vista, and the MS IM Private Credit & Equity seat in the table above.
