## The core thesis — now with the half that was missing

> [!important] Filter on the graduation band, not on a class year
> Expected graduation **Dec 2028 / Spring 2029** = **Class of 2029**. Programs gating on a graduation band (`Dec 2028 – June 2029`, `Class of 2029`) are yours. You hold ~3.52 GPA, an active **NJ real estate salesperson licence**, an active **life & health producer licence**, **first-generation** status, and Honors EDGE.

> [!danger] The trap cuts BOTH ways — this is new
> The known half: you are a **credit junior**, so sophomore standing-gated programs are closed. **The half that was missing from this file:** programs keyed to an *imminent* graduation are closed too. Blackstone's summer internship wants *"students in their junior year of college"* with a near-term graduation — with Spring 2029 you screen out as **two years early**, despite being a credit junior.
>
> So the filter is neither "sophomore" nor "junior." **Any sentence naming a class year as a requirement is a hard stop in either direction.** Band wording is the lane. That is precisely why Evercore works (*"a graduation date between December 2028 and June 2029"*, no standing clause) and Morgan Stanley Early Insights does not (*"second-year undergraduate students graduating between…"* — conjunctive).
