## The core thesis

> [!important] Grad-date band, not class standing
> Expected graduation **Spring 2029** = **Class of 2029**. Nearly every elite "sophomore" pipeline program gates on a graduation-date band (`Dec 2028 – June 2029`, `Class of 2029`, `Fall 2028 – Summer 2029`). You land inside those bands while holding:
> - ~3.52 GPA (Montclair, pre-transfer)
> - Active **NJ real estate salesperson license**
> - Active **life & health insurance producer license**
> - **First-generation** college student status
> - Honors EDGE program
>
> That is a materially weaker applicant pool than junior-year summer analyst recruiting. This is the window.

> [!warning] Hard line on standing-gated programs
> Some programs gate on *enrolled class standing*, not grad date — e.g. Capital One AEIP ("must be in Sophomore Undergraduate Standing"), Citi Freshman Discovery ("first-year student"). **You are not eligible.** Do not apply, do not self-describe as a sophomore. Offers run background and enrollment verification; a standing misstatement kills the offer and the relationship.

## What "automating the applications" can and can't mean

> [!caution] These cannot be auto-submitted
> Every one of these needs your login, your resume, your essays, and truthful self-reported enrollment data. Anything that auto-fills class standing or grad date across applications will eventually submit something false under your name.

**What is actually automatable:**
- Weekly scheduled scan of program pages for opening/closing dates → pushed to you
- A single master resume + 3 pre-cut variants (Consulting / RE-Finance / WM) so applications are copy-paste, not rewrite
- Calendar blocks auto-created 10 days before each expected deadline
- One canonical "about me" block reused across every portal

## Standing constraints

> [!danger] Name
> Every application, portal, and background check uses **current legal name (Joshua Bracero)** until the SSA update completes. Preferred-name fields can carry "Luke Salvatore." Do not let the change straddle a recruiting cycle.

> [!warning] GPA reset
> Montclair's 3.52 does **not** transfer as GPA. Rutgers GPA starts at zero in Spring 2027. Summer 2028 SA applications open ~Jan 2027 — meaning several will be submitted with **one semester or less** of Rutgers grades visible. Spring 2027 is the recruiting transcript, not just the RBS-gate transcript.

> [!info] SAS vs RBS
> You apply to everything above as an **SAS student** — RBS admission isn't until Fall 2028, after the decisive Summer 2028 internship. Confirm whether SAS students get access to RBS career services, employer info sessions, and SMF recruitment. If not, that's a real gap to route around early.
